Privacy Policy

Qurb: Prayer Times & Quran

Effective Date: November 1, 2026
Last Updated: November 1, 2026

Qurb: Prayer Times & Quran ("the App") is developed to provide prayer times, Qibla direction, Quranic content, Islamic resources, reminders, and related features.

This Privacy Policy explains how the App accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use it.

1. User Accounts and Personal Information

The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number to use its main features.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information for user registration or maintain user profiles.

2. Location Information

The App may request approximate or precise location permission when you use location-based features, including prayer times and Qibla direction.

Depending on the feature, your location coordinates or location search query may be sent to the following third-party services:

  • Aladhan API: Receives latitude, longitude, and prayer calculation settings to calculate prayer times.
  • BigDataCloud: Receives latitude and longitude for reverse geocoding to identify your city or country.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API: Receives city search queries to find available locations.

Your selected location and prayer calculation preferences are stored locally on your device. Prayer time results may also be cached locally in an SQLite database, including the coordinates used for the calculations.

The App does not require a user account for these features and does not intentionally store your location on its own remote servers.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and data retention practices.

3. Camera Access – AR Qibla Finder

The App may request camera permission when you use the AR Qibla Finder feature.

The camera provides a live preview with an augmented reality overlay to help indicate the Qibla direction.

The App does not provide functionality to capture or record photos or videos through this feature. Camera footage is not intentionally stored or transmitted by the App.

4. Photos and Media – Saving Generated Images

The App allows you to save app-generated images, such as Quranic Ayah cards and other visual content, to your device's Pictures/Qurb folder.

On Android 10 and later, the App uses Android's MediaStore system to save these images. On Android 9 and earlier, it may use the device's external storage functionality and the required storage permission.

This feature is intended to save images generated by the App. The App does not require access to your existing personal photos or documents for this functionality.

5. Local Storage and Preferences

The App stores certain information locally on your device to provide its features and remember your preferences.

This may include:

  • Selected location and prayer calculation preferences.
  • Prayer time results and related cached information.
  • Bookmarks and saved content.
  • Tasbeeh counters and worship planner progress.
  • App preferences and widget-related information.

This information is stored using local device storage, including SharedPreferences and SQLite where applicable.

You can generally remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's data through your device settings or uninstalling the App. Uninstalling or clearing data may permanently remove locally stored information.

6. Notifications and Reminders

The App may schedule local notifications and reminders, including prayer-time alerts, spiritual reminders, and daily planner task reminders.

These notifications are scheduled and managed locally on your device using Android's notification and alarm functionality. The App may request notification and exact alarm permissions where required for these features.

The App may also use Android's boot-related functionality to restore scheduled reminders after a device restart.

These local notifications do not require a remote push notification service to be scheduled.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

7. Device Sensors

The App uses the device's compass sensors, including magnetometer and orientation readings, to help determine the Qibla direction.

Compass readings are processed locally on your device to update the Qibla indicator. The App does not intentionally transmit compass readings to its servers or third parties.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may communicate with third-party services to provide its features and content. These include:

  • Aladhan API: Provides prayer time calculations using location coordinates and calculation settings.
  • AlQuran Cloud API: Provides Quranic text and translations.
  • BigDataCloud: Processes location coordinates for reverse geocoding.
  • Open-Meteo Geocoding API: Processes city search queries to find locations.
  • UmmahAPI: Provides Islamic duas and related content through a content request. The App does not intentionally include personal information or location coordinates in this request.
  • Mufti Maaz Haroon Website: Provides website content, articles, books, lectures, images, and related information.
  • YouTube: Provides video content, embedded video playback, and video thumbnails. The App may use YouTube's privacy-enhanced embedded player.
  • WhatsApp: Opens WhatsApp through user-initiated sharing links.

When you access these services, relevant information such as location coordinates, search queries, or content requests may be transmitted to the respective service.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Their data handling and retention practices are governed by their respective policies.

9. Advertising and Analytics

The App is not designed to display third-party advertisements or conduct behavioral advertising.

Based on the App components reviewed, no dedicated advertising or analytics SDK integration was identified. However, third-party services accessed by the App may process technical information as part of providing their services.

10. Data Security

The App communicates with its identified third-party services using HTTPS endpoints.

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information processed through the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information stored locally is subject to the security of your device and its operating system.

11. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The App does not require account registration or knowingly request personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App, its features, or applicable requirements.

Any updates will be published on the page where this Privacy Policy is made available. The updated date at the top of this document will indicate when the latest changes were made.
